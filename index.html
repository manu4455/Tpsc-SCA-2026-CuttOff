"Est. total appeared" is your best guess at how many candidates actually sat the written exam in this category — not how many have logged a score here. The prediction scales your logged sample against this number, since a register of ~100 entries is only a slice of the real exam-taking population. Adjust it as better information becomes available.

#ScoreCategoryLogged
How to read this: entries are self-reported against the written exam (85 marks) and not verified against any official answer key. The prediction works by percentile, not raw rank: TPSC calls a fixed top slice of each category (1 vacancy \u2192 5 candidates, 2 \u2192 8, 3 or more \u2192 3\u00d7 vacancies, applied here per category using the combined vacancy count across all three departments). That slice is converted into a percentage of however many candidates you estimate actually appeared, then that same percentage is applied to your logged sample to find the predicted score \u2014 this avoids the mistake of reading the raw Nth-highest score off a small sample as if the sample were the whole applicant pool. The "estimated total appeared" number is a manual guess since TPSC hasn't published it; the prediction is only as good as that guess and your sample size. TPSC also calls any candidate tied with the last qualifying score, which this tool doesn't model. Department is assigned later by merit and stated preference, and vacancy numbers are subject to change if departments revise their requisition. The final merit list combines written (85) and interview (15) marks. Check tpsc.tripura.gov.in/cut-off-marks for the official figure once released.
